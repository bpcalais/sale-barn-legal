Delete your Sale Barn Prices account

For the app Sale Barn Prices, by Calais, LLC

You can delete your Sale Barn Prices account and the data tied to it at any time. Choose whichever is easier for you.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open Sale Barn Prices and tap the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete account to confirm.

The deletion happens immediately and can’t be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can’t open the app (for example, you have uninstalled it or forgotten your password), email us from the address on your account:

To: bp.calaispet@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my Sale Barn Prices account

We will confirm that the request came from the account’s email address and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We’ll reply once it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in details (including your password, which we stored in scrambled form).
  • Your favorite barns.
  • Your notification settings and device registrations.

What we may keep

The market prices shown in the app come from public reports and aren’t tied to you, so they remain. Copies of technical logs or backups held by our hosting providers are removed on the providers’ normal schedules, which are limited in time. We don’t keep any other personal information about you after deletion.

Questions? Read our privacy policy or email bp.calaispet@gmail.com.
