Privacy Policy

Sale Barn Prices, operated by Calais, LLC · Last updated November 7, 2026

Sale Barn Prices (“the app”) shows livestock market prices gathered from public market reports published by sale barns and cooperatives. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Short version: you can browse prices without an account. If you create one, we keep your email address, your password (stored scrambled, so we can’t read it), and the barns you mark as favorites. We don’t sell your information. You can delete your account and its data at any time, inside the app or by email (see Deleting your account).

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or payment information. We don’t use analytics or tracking tools in the app at this time.

The market prices themselves come from public reports and are not personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and show your favorite barns.
  • To send account emails: a confirmation code when you sign up and a code when you reset your password.
  • To send notifications about new reports from barns you follow, if you turn them on.
  • To keep the app secure, fix problems, and respond to your messages.

We don’t use your information for advertising profiles, and we don’t sell it.

3. Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run the app. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in system (your email, hashed password, and favorites).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers notifications to your device, if you allow them.
  • An email delivery service: sends the confirmation and password-reset emails to your address.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety and rights of our users and the service. If the app is ever sold or transferred, your information may transfer with it, and we will tell you if your choices change.

4. Sponsor banners

The app may show a banner from a sponsor at the top of the screen. We don’t give sponsors your email address or your favorites. If we begin using an advertising network that collects device identifiers, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your email address, password, favorites, and notification details are removed from our active database right away. Copies in provider logs or backups are removed on the providers’ normal schedules, which are limited in time.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time:

  • In the app: open the Account tab, tap Delete account, and enter your password to confirm.
  • By email: if you can’t use the app, write to bp.calaispet@gmail.com from the email address on the account, and we will delete it within 30 days. Full steps are on our account deletion page.

7. Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and passwords are stored in hashed form. No online service is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can look at, correct, or delete your information by using the app or by contacting us. You can turn notifications off in your phone’s settings at any time. Depending on where you live (for example California, the European Union, or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, such as receiving a copy of your information or objecting to how it is used. To use any of these rights, email us at bp.calaispet@gmail.com. We don’t sell personal information and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults who follow livestock markets. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where information is processed

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
